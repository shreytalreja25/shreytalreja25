> *Currently driving technology solutions & enterprise engineering as a Consultant at [StatusNeo](https://statusneo.com) — while building AI-native tools, multi-agent frameworks, and creative computing systems on the side.*

I am a **Technologist, AI-Native Full-Stack Engineer, and Systems Architect**. I operate across the modern compute spectrum—from designing low-latency inference pipelines and multi-agent orchestrations, to architecting distributed web platforms and real-time graphics environments. My engineering philosophy centers on **rigorous determinism over stochastic ambiguity**: converting bleeding-edge research into observable, robust, enterprise-grade software.

### 💡 Core Engineering Tenets

> *"Premature abstraction is as dangerous as unmodular monoliths. Build with sharp boundaries, verified contracts, and deterministic evaluations."*

- **Deterministic Verification over Black-Box Assumptions:** Generative models are only as capable as the evaluation harnesses, confidence scoring, and guardrails wrapped around them.
- **Performance as a Feature:** Latency budgets, memory profiles, and zero extraneous render cycles form the baseline of scalable architecture.
- **Maintainable by Design:** Codebases should be strongly typed end-to-end, modularly decoupled, and reinforced with automated CI/CD validation.
